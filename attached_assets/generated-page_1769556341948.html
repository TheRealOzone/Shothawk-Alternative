Why Us?

Founded by a group of 3 frustrated students, ShotHawk was a testament to creating safer spaces by combining engineering and determination. Our mission is to provide an autonomous threat mitigation device to protect schools and businesses. We are comitted to providing children and families a peace of mind by eliminating gun and knife threats one building at a time.

Today, we have the honor to be pilot testing in 15 varying locations throughout the State of Illinois in partnership with the Illinois Science and Technology Coalition. With every install, we see our vision of enabling children and communities to be safe; moreover, by 2030, we see ShotHawk as an installed countermeasure to Active Threat Situations in ALL buildings serving ALL communities.
